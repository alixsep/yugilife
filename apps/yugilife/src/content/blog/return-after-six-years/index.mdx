Yugilife was originally created in 2019. Back then, there was no ChatGPT, and I wrote the entire project myself (excluding a few pieces copied from Stack Overflow; I even posted a question there when I was seeking help with [x-axis scaling for the title of the cards](https://stackoverflow.com/questions/61533780/how-to-use-css-transform-scale-to-fit-child-div-to-parent-div-responsively-i)). Every single character, even braces (`{`), brackets (`[`), and so on, was written by me pressing physical keys on my keyboard. I owned what I made and managed to finish a decent product for the Yu-Gi-Oh! community.

And it was rewarding. It felt genuinely good to know that I was the only person on Earth who had created the best Yu-Gi-Oh! card creator website. There was [a post on Reddit](https://www.reddit.com/r/customyugioh/comments/1etxdu8/which_one_of_these_cards_is_the_best_visually/) asking something like, "Which card generator is the best?" and the poll recommended mine. (Extra note: If you open it, the poll shows Yugilife as the least-voted-for option and Duelingbook as the most-voted-for option, but the original poster's comment said: "Oh my god I just realized I put Dueling Book and Yugilife opposite to each other." This means Yugilife was the one that got the most votes.)

And then I left the project because of Covid. Covid completely destroyed my social life and I was depressed. I was quarantined at home for about 1.5 years and after that I spent another 1.5 years barely leaving home. All my friends were on Discord and English-speaking. I was only studying, making music occasionally, and talking with my online friends.

In 2022, I was working on Yugilife again, adding more polish, better looks, and improved performance with the programming skills and knowledge I had recently acquired. However, it required too much effort, and because it was the final semester of my bachelor's degree and I was preparing for my master's degree, I was under strict time constraints and unfortunately had to leave development of the new version halfway through.

And many more. Many, many more. I feel happy that my being makes a positive difference, even if it's as small as letting people create Yu-Gi-Oh! cards. There are at least 100 emails like these in my inbox. At the same time, it was a sharp pain in my chest every time I received such emails, knowing that I wasn't working on this project even though all these people looked up to me and I knew exactly how to make it much better.

At the time of writing, I'm finishing my master's degree, and a lot of crazy things have happened. I'm Iranian and live in Tehran. Due to the horrible economy, protests took place on January 8 and 9, 2026, and credible estimates put the civilian death toll at 40,000 to 60,000. The government cut off our access to the global internet for a whopping 88 (eighty fucking eight) days in a row. This is absurd, given that other parts of the world are working on humanoid robotics and developing new AI models while we were literally just wishing we could access Google again! Man what the hell I couldn't even listen to music! I'm not trying to bring politics into this blog post; I just wanted to explain the situation I was stuck in, but ultimately, I didn't let it stop me.

I gathered all the materials and leftovers I had accumulated since 2013 and spent several weeks working on Yugilife for at least 12 hours every single day without exception, rebuilding it from the ground up. I managed to create this version, which is a night-and-day difference from the original version. Many design choices were made differently, but those deserve their own blog posts. It's surprising how big of a project this has become given that, on the surface, it seems trivial. I promise it isn't.

I'm currently planning to migrate to the EU for my PhD and it's important to me to do this legally, since the EU is facing its own immigration challenges and I want to live and contribute there constructively. This is a complex topic, which I will explain at some point, but not in a Yugilife blog post, since it is outside the scope of this project. To be honest, I am still in a very stressful situation, and failing to migrate would ruin all the plans I have made for a meaningful future for myself and the wider community. I'm not ready for another round of dealing with depression due to hopelessness. So I will try to keep my head up and spend my time doing things that bring value and happiness to both me and the world.

I'm looking forward to helping the community by understanding their needs better.
I also look forward to meeting people in the community who might be able to help me with the challenges I face, although I am neither seeking nor expecting that.
